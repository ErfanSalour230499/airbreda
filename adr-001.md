# ADR-001: Initial Data Storage Strategy

## Status
Accepted

## Context
AirBreda ingests hourly NO2 readings from Luchtmeetnet (NL10240) and live traffic
counts from four NDW sites on the A27. Volume is small (about 100,000 rows per year).

## Decision
- Parsed sensor readings go into Azure Database for PostgreSQL.
- Raw NDW files go into Azure Blob Storage (container `ndw`).
- Duplicates are handled with a primary key on (station_id, timestamp, component)
  and `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so every write is idempotent
  (at-least-once delivery with idempotent writes).

## Why SQL and not NoSQL
The data is structured, we need time-range queries and joins between air quality
and traffic, and the volume is tiny. NoSQL scale is not a problem we have.

## Why object storage for raw files
It is cheap and durable, it is an audit trail, and it lets us re-parse the data and
retrain the model later.

## Alternative rejected
Cosmos DB (Azure's DynamoDB equivalent): more expensive, weak at joins, and
unnecessary for this volume.

## Consequences and risks
- The database firewall is open to all IPs for the lab. Must be restricted later.
- Region limited to France Central by the student subscription policy.
