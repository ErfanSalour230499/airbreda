How to read this page

NO₂ is a gas from engines. Low is under 20 µg/m³, elevated is 20 to 40, and above 40 passes the EU annual limit value, which we use here only as an hourly reference. Traffic is the hourly mean number of vehicles per hour at four sensors (hrl and hrr are the two A27 directions, vwd and vwa are the slip roads). The model is a linear regression on total traffic and hour of day. Exceedance risk is a 0 to 1 score, not a calibrated probability. With so little training data, treat predictions as a demonstration of the pipeline, not as a forecast.
